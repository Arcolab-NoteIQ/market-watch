FDA Form 483s and
warning letters

The latest Form 483s listed on FDA483s.com (Redica), FDA's own Reading Room postings and recent CDER warning letters. Newest inspection first.

FDA posts redacted 483s in batches, often long after the inspection. Full 483 text on Redica is paid, so synopses come from company stock-exchange disclosures and trade press (linked as Source); they report what the company said, not FDA's wording.

How signal is rated
  • High: acts on a deal or our positioning now. A named direct competitor launches, acquires, raises money or wins a customer in our exact space, or a regulation or enforcement action changes what clients must buy or do.
  • Medium: shapes the pitch, but nothing to act on this week. Adjacent competitors or big platforms adding features, and industry trends useful in messaging.
  • Low: background context. General pharma AI deals, drug discovery news, market-size reports and rankings of firms we do not compete with head on.

Ratings and "why it matters" notes are written by Claude from public sources. Open the source link before using any figure in client material.
